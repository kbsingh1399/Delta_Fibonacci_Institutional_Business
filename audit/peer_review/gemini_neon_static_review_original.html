Hold live capital: six structural defects invalidate the reported backtest edge

An independent line-by-line code and statistical audit of the Delta Fibonacci Institutional Business repository across simulation mechanics, Deflated Sharpe validation, price-action detectors, and MT5 live execution.

Investment committee verdict: Reject for production deployment until remediated. Four of five audit domains contain severe mathematical or execution-parity defects. Most critically, 80% of the 25 selected setups rely on trailing stops that collect phantom profit in backtests and are silently replaced by static 2.5R targets in live trading.

Backtest engine inflates trailing stops and omits costs from R

Inspecting simulate_with_signals (lines 181–335 of engine/multidim_grid_backtest.py) confirms that bar-to-bar signal evaluation avoids direct future-close peeking: signals fire on closed bar i and enter at o[i + 1], with SL/TP checked starting on bar i + 1. However, four concrete simulation bugs severely overstate performance:

1. Post-hoc trailing ratchet creates phantom-profit fills

Lines 277–286 update pos_sl at the end of bar i using the bar's peak excursion (pos_mfe). If a long trade spikes to +2.0R intrabar and reverses to close at +0.2R (without hitting the original stop), the engine ratchets pos_sl to +1.5R after the bar has already closed at +0.2R. On bar i + 1, which opens at +0.2R, line 251 sees touch_price <= pos_sl and fills the exit at pos_sl = +1.5R instead of o[i + 1] = +0.2R. Because 20 of 25 champions use trail_* exits, this single bug systematically manufactures profit on spike-and-reverse bars.

2. Intrabar adverse sequencing is optimistic on half of all bars

Lines 242–245 set seq = [("LOW", bar_l), ("HIGH", bar_h)] if bullish else [("HIGH", bar_h), ("LOW", bar_l)]. That logic is not universally adverse: for a long position on a bearish bar (c[i] < o[i]), the loop evaluates HIGH before LOW, awarding TP_HIT before testing SL_HIT. Symmetrically, for a short position on a bullish bar, it evaluates LOW before HIGH. True worst-case sequencing must test SL before TP based strictly on pos_side, never bar polarity.

3. Commission is excluded from R-multiples and spread/slippage is zero

Line 291 computes exit_r = (exit_p - pos_entry) / pos_rd and only deducts commission_usd = 0.05 from pnl_usd on line 292. Every downstream metric—net_r, profit_factor, expectancy_r, Monte Carlo, and DSR—uses raw, cost-free r_multiple. Even if deducted, $0.05 on $5.00 risk (0.01R) ignores bid-ask spread, stop-out slippage, gap-through fills (filling gapped stops at exact pos_sl rather than o[i]), and overnight swaps on 1H/4H holds. On 5M DJ30.p (0.047R/trade) or 15M EURUSD.pi (0.051R/trade), realistic 0.08R–0.15R round-trip friction erases the entire edge.

4. Session VWAP anchor drifts during DST and relies on tick volume

In engine/vwap_bands.py, anchor_mode == "broker" shifts timestamps by a hardcoded +2 hours. Broker NY-close servers alternate between UTC+2 (winter) and UTC+3 (summer), causing VWAP session resets to drift by one hour for half the year. Additionally, tick_volume counts broker quote updates rather than transacted volume, making standard deviation bands broker-dependent.

Deflated Sharpe implementation breaks Bailey & López de Prado and has an OR backdoor

A line-by-line review of engine/quant_robustness.py and scripts/run_quant_validation_and_monte_carlo.py shows that the statistical validation layer does not protect against selection bias.

Four mathematical errors in DSR

  • Annualized vs. per-trade unit mismatch: sr is scaled by sqrt(min(max(n, 10), 252)), then plugged into the non-annualized Sharpe variance formula, inflating sr ** 2 by up to 252x while comparing against an unannualized benchmark.
  • Severe under-counting of trials: run_quant_validation_and_monte_carlo.py passes n_trials=40, whereas optimize_multidim_basket.py sweeps 4 TFs × 5 models × 9 exits = 180 trials per symbol (4,140 across 23 symbols).
  • Full-sample contamination: Both DSR and the 10,000-path Monte Carlo run on full_trades (IS + OOS combined) instead of strict OOS trades.
  • Post-hoc 70/30 split: optimize_multidim_basket.py selects winning parameters on 100% of bars before run_quant_validation_and_monte_carlo.py slices 70/30, invalidating OOS independence.

Why IID Monte Carlo understates tail risk

_simulate_bootstrap_paths_numba resamples individual trades IID with replacement (np.random.randint(0, n_trades)). In mean-reversion VWAP/EMA systems, losses cluster heavily during trending macro regimes and volatility shocks. IID resampling breaks autocorrelation and systematically underestimates losing streaks.

Even under optimistic IID sampling, 8 of 25 champions exceed the stated 50R DD_95% limit (up to 73.93R on DJ30.p 5M), and 17 of 25 carry a ≥ 50% probability of hitting a 20R drawdown. Replace IID sampling with a Stationary Block Bootstrap (geometrically distributed block lengths, mean block size L ≈ n^(1/3)) or regime-conditioned Markov sampling, plus synchronized portfolio-level resampling to capture cross-asset correlation spikes across SP500, DJ30, NAS100, and crypto CFDs.

Price action detectors suffer from dead logic and unnormalized regime thresholds

Inspecting engine/candle_rejection.py reveals that the five rejection variants share rigid magic numbers calibrated without regard to asset-class microstructure, tick size, or volatility regime.

Code-level pattern flaws

  • Redundant pinbar close filter: In detect_rejection_wick (lines 22–23), lower_wick = (min(o, c) - l) / rng >= 0.35 mathematically implies (c - l) / rng >= 0.35. The second clause is dead code, allowing a bearish-close candle with a 36% lower wick and 60% upper wick to fire a bullish pinbar.
  • Unnormalized 5 bps EMA slope filter: check_ema_rejection_signal requires ema_val >= ema_prev * 0.9995. A flat 5 bps tolerance is trivially satisfied on 5M FX bars (where typical bar-to-bar EMA drift is < 1 bp) while acting as a tight hurdle on 4H crypto.
  • Pattern conflation & priority shadowing: detect_two_bar_reversal and detect_three_bar_sweep overlap on nearly identical bar-i reclaim rules, and evaluate_level_rejection short-circuits in fixed order, preventing isolated attribution of each variant's incremental alpha.

Regime-adaptive volatility calibration

Fixed tolerances (tol = 0.20 * atr, 0.15 * atr in VWAP checks, risk_dist >= 1.2 * atr, and a 1e-5 floor that distorts low-nominal-price crypto like XLMUSD) fail at both ends of the volatility spectrum:

  • Low-volatility drift: 14-period Wilder ATR compresses until 1.2 * ATR approaches broker spread width, causing spread-noise stop-outs. Floor tolerances using live spread: max(alpha * ATR_14, k * spread_live) with k >= 4.
  • High-volatility flash events: Trailing ATR lags sudden expansion and stays bloated afterward. Scale tolerances by the volatility ratio ATR_14 / ATR_100 (or Garman-Klass realized volatility percentile) and replace fixed 0.35 wick ratios with asset-specific rolling Z-scores of wick-to-range ratios.

Live MT5 daemon diverges from backtests and lacks margin governance

The widest operational gap sits inside engine/live_multicriteria_runner.py. Even if the historical backtest were bug-free, the live daemon executes a fundamentally different strategy:

  • Zero trailing-stop execution: Lines 231–240 map elif "2.0" in exit_mode or "trail" in exit_mode: target_rr = 2.5 and send a single static SL/TP market order. There is no breakeven ratchet or trailing-stop management loop anywhere in the daemon—every trail_0.5r, trail_1.0r, and trail_1.5r champion (20 of 25 setups) is silently capped at 2.5R, and fixed_2.0r is misrouted to 2.5R.
  • Hardcoded minimum lot sizing: Although calculate_position_lot_size is imported on line 33, line 219 hardcodes volume = float(info.volume_min). Dollar risk per trade is completely unnormalized across assets and stop widths, invalidating equal-R portfolio math.
  • Latency guard blind spots: Computing elapsed_since_close = tick.time - forming_bar_open_time relies on the broker's last quote timestamp rather than monotonic UTC wall-clock time. During feed freezes or weekend/rollover boundaries, a stale tick.time can appear fresh; conversely, on illiquid pairs (GBPNOK.pi, HK50.p), a 30-second first-tick delay silently drops valid 5M signals. Add a dual guard: abs(server_now - tick.time) <= 10s and a live spread filter (spread / risk_dist <= 0.15).
  • Margin starvation on 1:2 Crypto CFDs: The daemon performs zero pre-trade mt5.order_check() or free-margin checks. One XRPUSD min-lot at $483 margin (1:2 leverage) can exhaust free margin and trigger NO_MONEY rejections on high-Sharpe FX/Index setups. Implement a Margin-Budgeted Capital Allocator with (1) hard free-margin floor (margin_level >= 300%), (2) asset-class margin caps (max 20% in Crypto CFDs), (3) Return-on-Margin (RoM) priority queuing, and (4) automatic skip whenever min_lot_risk > 1.5 × target_risk_usd.

Priority vulnerabilities and highest-leverage alpha upgrades

Remediation should freeze live deployment, repair simulator and statistical integrity first, and then layer portfolio-level risk and microstructure intelligence onto the surviving 1H/4H core.

Top 3 quantitative vulnerabilities

  1. Backtest-to-live trailing-stop breakdown & phantom fills: 80% of the basket relies on trail_* exits that collect impossible next-bar open fills in simulate_with_signals and are never executed in live_multicriteria_runner.py (silently capped at 2.5R). Fix gap-aware fills (min(o[i+1], pos_sl) for longs), enforce side-based adverse sequencing, deduct live-calibrated spread+slippage directly from r_multiple, and implement an asynchronous MT5 position-ratchet loop.
  2. Post-hoc OOS splitting & bypassed DSR: Selecting champions on 100% of data in optimize_multidim_basket.py before the 70/30 split—combined with the broken 0.40 DSR formula, n_trials=40 undercount, and OR bypass—invalidates all 25 "verified" setups. Move to anchored/rolling Purged K-Fold / Walk-Forward Optimization where parameter selection occurs strictly inside each training fold, and enforce true Bailey & López de Prado DSR (≥ 0.95) on OOS returns only.
  3. Unnormalized min-lot sizing & unguarded margin exhaustion: Trading info.volume_min without pre-trade mt5.order_check() breaks constant-R expectancy and allows 1:2 leverage Crypto CFDs to starve higher-Sharpe FX/Index signals. Activate volatility-targeted lot sizing with hard per-class margin caps and a skip rule when min_lot_risk > 1.5 × target_risk_usd.

Top 3 high-impact next-stage alpha additions

  1. Hierarchical Risk Parity (HRP) & cross-asset correlation throttling: The basket stacks highly correlated risk-on bets (SP500, DJ30, NAS100, BTCUSD, ETHUSD, XRPUSD, XLMUSD). Computing rolling 60-day return covariance and capping concurrent gross beta exposure prevents synchronized macro stop-outs that IID Monte Carlo fails to predict.
  2. Explicit market-regime gating (Trend vs. Mean-Reversion): Mean-reversion VWAP SD-band fades bleed during high-ADX / macro breakout regimes. Conditioning entry eligibility on rolling Hurst exponent, ADX(14), or realized volatility percentile (ATR_14 / ATR_100) suppresses counter-trend bleed without adding fragile candle parameters.
  3. True order-flow & microstructure confirmation (Volume Delta / Spread-to-ATR): Replace broker tick-volume VWAP proxies with tick-rule signed delta (Lee-Ready / uptick-downtick cumulative volume delta divergence at structural bands) paired with a hard execution filter rejecting entries when live spread exceeds 12–15% of stop distance.
